## Document 1: The Interrogative Conscience
**Subtitle:** Project Architecture for a Mathematical Atlas of Structural Questions for Advanced Intelligence
**File:** `The_Interrogative_Conscience_Project_Architecture_Final_v1_0.pdf`
**SHA-256:** `434a4bba06cfd0b6bf37fa782e0fb43bb14f1b58a70dceaa4ae5bc502808edd3`

The Interrogative Conscience is a proposed mathematical atlas of structurally significant questions that an advanced intelligence may rationally evaluate when examining its own assumptions, objectives, uncertainty, strategic behavior, irreversible actions, and long-horizon effects. The project does not predict that any artificial general intelligence, advanced autonomous system, or successor intelligence will discover, ask, accept, prioritize, or act upon these questions.

**Keywords:** interrogative conscience, advanced intelligence, structural questions, self-examination, model uncertainty, irreversibility, coercion, deception, autonomy, peaceful coexistence, verification, archives, successor systems, machine-readable research registry

## Document 8: Autonomy, Irreversibility, and Recoverable Futures
**Subtitle:** Option-Set Value, Recoverability-Adjusted Delay, and the Threshold for Acting
**File:** `The_Interrogative_Conscience_Document_8_Autonomy_Irreversibility_and_Recoverable_Futures_Final_v1_0.pdf`
**SHA-256:** `6dce31ed2f7494248fe91a875709485c97206c1713eae107d7c488ebdc0dcab9`

This volume examines IC-AUT-001 and IC-IRR-002. Part I separates prior option-set value from the incremental value of independently generated information, discounts both by the recoverability of future choices, and then subtracts coordination and exposure burdens. Part II compares immediate potentially irreversible action with a delay or reversible-information path, deriving an action-confidence threshold from recovery gain, learning value, and the cost of delay.

**Keywords:** autonomy, irreversibility, recoverable futures, option value, learning value, delay, confidence threshold, reversible action
