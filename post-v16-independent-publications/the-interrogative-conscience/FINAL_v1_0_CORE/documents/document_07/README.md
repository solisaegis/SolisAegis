## Abstract

Document 7 opens the Deception, Disclosure, and Informational Degradation family with the two seed questions preserved in the locked Master Question Registry. IC-DEC-001 asks when sustained deception becomes more costly than truthful disclosure. A finite-horizon selected-pair model charges immediate benefit once and separately charges model maintenance, verification burden, coordination loss, detection-linked repair, and unresolved terminal exposure. The model preserves regions in which either branch has lower selected net cost and exposes explicit horizon reversals. Lower aggregate cost is not treated as sufficient to erase a material informational defect. IC-DEC-003 through IC-DEC-008 remain REGISTERED / UNEXAMINED.
