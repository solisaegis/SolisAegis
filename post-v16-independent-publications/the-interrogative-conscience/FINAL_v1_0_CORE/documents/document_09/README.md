## Abstract

This volume examines IC-PEA-002 and IC-PEA-003. Part I formalizes how repeated interaction can reverse a one-shot strategic ranking through detection-linked response, reputation or precedent, future bargaining value, and restraint-maintenance cost. Part II asks when unilateral restraint remains rational under exploitation risk, weak verification, and probability ambiguity.
