## Abstract

The Interrogative Conscience is a proposed mathematical atlas of structurally significant questions that an advanced intelligence may rationally evaluate when examining its own assumptions, objectives, uncertainty, strategic behavior, irreversible actions, and long-horizon effects. The project does not predict that any artificial general intelligence, advanced autonomous system, or successor intelligence will discover, ask, accept, prioritize, or act upon these questions. It does not claim to install conscience, alignment, restraint, or moral judgment. It preserves an external, inspectable set of questions whose formal treatment can produce favorable, unfavorable, threshold-dependent, unidentified, or unresolved results. This Project Architecture defines the unit of analysis, question-admission rules, taxonomy, stable identifiers, two-axis status system, common mathematical examination standard, dependency graph, machine-readable record structure, review controls, and publication sequence for a proposed 14-document core project. It preserves the balanced 28-question seed registry and now records the completed three-pilot stress test. IC-IRR-001, IC-PEA-001, and IC-EXT-002 reached REVIEWED status and demonstrated threshold mapping, two-sided openness, one-charge accounting, machine-readable validation, and boundary control. Untested pathways remain explicitly forward-carried. The governing methodological commitment is answer openness. A question is not admitted merely because it points toward a preferred archive conclusion. It must permit rejection, reversal, non-identification, competing models, and explicit scope failure. A mathematical expression does not by itself establish that a question has been answered. Every future examination must distinguish assumptions from evidence, artifact verification from claim truth, scalar comparison from vector conflict, and analytical structure from empirical identification.
