## Abstract

This volume examines IC-PLU-001 and IC-EXT-001. Part I compares preservation of an independent disagreement signal with immediate convergence or aggregation, using common-mode error, residual independence, signal quality, correction value, option value, coordination cost, and false-alarm burden. Part II compares sole self-review with an independent external-check branch under uncertainty about self-compromise, reviewer capture or correlation, corrective value, false intervention, review cost, and appeal or reversibility. Both results are threshold-dependent and preserve regions favorable to either branch.
