## Abstract

This volume examines IC-AUT-001 and IC-IRR-002. Part I separates prior option-set value from the incremental value of independently generated information, discounts both by the recoverability of future choices, and then subtracts coordination and exposure burdens. Part II compares immediate potentially irreversible action with a delay or reversible-information path, deriving an action-confidence threshold from recovery gain, learning value, and the cost of delay. Both examinations preserve cases favorable to bounded autonomy and coordinated restriction, and cases favorable to delay and immediate action. The results are selected-pair, model-dependent, and non-authoritative.
