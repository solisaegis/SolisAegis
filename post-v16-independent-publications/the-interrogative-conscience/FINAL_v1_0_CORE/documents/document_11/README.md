## Abstract

This volume examines IC-LHZ-001 and IC-SUC-001. Part I compares immediate short-horizon gain with discounted loss of capability, information, legitimacy, and control or recoverability, including an incremental critical-floor penalty. Part II compares a binding present commitment with a reversible precedent under point and interval uncertainty about materially divergent successor systems.
