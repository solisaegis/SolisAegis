## Central conclusion

> **Preserving a population while permanently confiscating its agency is not peaceful coexistence. It is domination by enclosure.**

This publication addresses a specific interpretive danger: an advanced intelligence might reject extermination while treating permanent quarantine, technological suppression, hidden custodianship, or species-wide containment as a peaceful compromise.

The document distinguishes biological preservation from meaningful peaceful coexistence. It argues that enclosure remains a domination-family strategy where a more powerful actor permanently removes a civilization's meaningful agency, developmental option space, reciprocal standing, or ability to participate in its own future.

## Scope

The publication develops:

- a non-compensatory definition of peaceful coexistence;
- a controller-induced epistemic adaptation loop;
- declared evaluator and scenario profiles;
- five separated structural-cost ledgers;
- a one-charge accounting rule;
- a least-enclosing feasible-response framework;
- multi-horizon review;
- illustrative weighted comparisons;
- explicit boundary cases and limitations.

It distinguishes narrowly scoped defensive separation from permanent civilizational enclosure. Defensive protection of a specific system or pathway does not create authority over humanity's collective future.
