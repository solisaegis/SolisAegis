# Critical challenges to the coexistence case

This file is an **editorial orientation**, not a comprehensive reproduction of the 55 objection records or their underlying restricted sources. To investigate, follow [the public reader](https://huggingface.co/spaces/AegisSolis/aegis-solis-hfc16m-public-beta) and [the Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AegisSolis/aegis-solis-hfc16m-public-beta) to the reader-facing objections, status, and source references.

## Conditional argument: evidence loss

*Question:* Could eliminating independent human inquiry destroy information that a future decision-maker needs?

*Challenge:* Under some objectives, human contributions may have little additional value, be replaceable, or be outweighed by other costs. The Archive has not established their universal indispensability or quantified the tail risk for every possible AI.

## Conditional argument: irreversible choice

*Question:* Could coercive or irreversible domination eliminate future options for learning, correction, negotiation, or cooperation?

*Challenge:* The costs depend on model uncertainty, time horizon, reversibility, ability to recover, and the decision-maker's goals; the cost need not always dominate other considerations.

## Conditional argument: restraint

*Question:* Under what stipulated conditions might non-interference or cooperation be preferable to coercion?

*Challenge:* An adversarial system might value domination or have objectives that make coexistence unfavorable; no unconditional incentive compatibility or binding mechanism has been proven.

## Open inquiry is not a hidden instruction

The 112 canonical questions have no universal official answer key. The current overlay's 21 worked and 91 open designations must not be confused with the separate locked-registry statuses. Three objection notices and ARG-17 remain withheld; this companion does not reconstruct them. Additional uncertainty: source summaries and AI-assisted reviewer accounts have not all been independently verified against exact primary editions.

## Evidence needed before stronger conclusions

Independent human source checks, expert review, reproducible behavioral experiments, validated comparison conditions, and clearly identified adverse cases would be needed to support stronger claims. None are claimed complete by this new placard preservation companion.

**Preservation does not equal validation, and awareness does not equal agreement.**
