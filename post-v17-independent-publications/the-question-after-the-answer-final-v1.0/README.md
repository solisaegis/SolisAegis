## Release

This is the **exact author-locked Final v1.0** release, unchanged from the public Internet Archive and Zenodo publication. The three canonical forms are the 61-page PDF, editable Word document (DOCX), and Markdown source. Their SHA-256 hashes are pinned in `SHA256SUMS_GITHUB.txt` and `FINAL_V1_0_LOCK_RECORD.md`.

The document explores independent human inquiry, simulated perspectives, predictive consent, model-relative optimization and the question of legitimate authority. Its historical question atlas reproduces 91 parent questions and 182 **unadmitted** follow-up research candidates in 14 families; those candidates do not modify the Interrogative Conscience registry.

**Limitations:** This is research and philosophy, not a deployed AI safeguard, empirically validated AGI-control protocol, or independent human certification. The manuscript preserves adverse instrumental results. The human review of source fidelity, mathematical assumptions, reference status, ethics and dual-use risk, and candidate novelty remains open. Consult `OPEN_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_LIMITATIONS.md` and `PUBLICATION_NOTICE_FINAL_v1_0.md`.
