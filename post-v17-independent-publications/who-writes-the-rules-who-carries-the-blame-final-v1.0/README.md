## Research summary

This independent AI-assisted documentary synthesis examines who originates, approves, implements and reverses AI governance rules, who advises those decision makers, and who carries responsibility when a decision affects people. It proposes a version-bound authority–accountability provenance crosswalk for linking advisory material, deployed technical versions, institutional claims, outcomes, and remedy rights.

The **11-case exploratory calibration yielded 5 NOT_EVALUABLE, 6 UNKNOWN and 0 determinate results**, and failed its complete-join feasibility check on the studied public-record frame. The manuscript retains adverse examples, backfire possibilities and open human/legal/empirical review gates.
