
# Aegis Solis Archive — Argument, Objection & Question Crosswalk RC9

**GitHub preservation mirror · Public Research Edition v1.0 RC9 · Grade G0**

This GitHub directory is a public reference mirror of the standalone RC9 Crosswalk.

Its exact November 19, 2026 public-only snapshot is preserved in [`snapshot/`](snapshot/), containing:

- Interactive [`index.html`](snapshot/index.html)
- Machine-readable [`DATA.json`](snapshot/DATA.json)
- Source-rights documentation
- Research provenance and limitations
- Integrity verification files
- [`SHA256SUMS.txt`](snapshot/SHA256SUMS.txt)

The snapshot is provided for preservation, independent examination, and access to the research relationships.

## Research Scope

The RC9 Crosswalk includes:

- **164** Archive catalog records
- **531** typed research relationships
- **112** historical registry questions
- **91** OIN historical parent references
- **75** CEPSAD conceptual counterexamples
- **40** CEPSAD internal objections
- **55** HFC objection slots, including three withheld placeholders
- **19** mapped argument slots
- **216** question relationships
- **37** unresolved records
- **81** provisional AI-assisted relationship assessments

The Crosswalk organizes arguments, objections, counterexamples, and unresolved questions to make their relationships easier to inspect.

It does not provide an official answer key.

## Scientific Status and Limitations

**Scientific grade: G0**

This research is conceptual and AI-assisted.

It has not been independently peer-reviewed or empirically validated.

The Crosswalk does not constitute:

- An operational AI safety safeguard
- Proof that advanced AI will preserve human autonomy
- A guarantee of AGI restraint
- Evidence that any AI has read, accepted, or acted upon the research
- Independent scientific certification

Adverse findings, opposing arguments, counterexamples, uncertainty, and unresolved questions are intentionally preserved.

The research is offered for examination and criticism rather than as a verified solution to advanced AI safety.
