# Crosswalk RC9 — Rights and source scope (author-approved Zenodo mirror)

**Publication companion note, 2026-11-16.** This file is a newly drafted companion note, **not** the original RC9 `PUBLIC_SCOPE_AND_LICENSE.md` and not legal certification. The static `index.html` is an editorial derivative of the hash-verified original RC9 reader. The website edition was authorized and published 2026-11-13; the author approved this public-only standalone mirror package for **Zenodo distribution** on 2026-11-16. This is an approval of publication scope, not a blanket change to the source licenses.

- **CEPSAD Final v1.0:** The author-owned original CEPSAD C1–C75 conceptual cases and Section 12 internal objection text appear with attribution and the work's **CC BY 4.0** terms. That license does not govern every component in the Crosswalk. Source: https://zenodo.org/records/23845157
- **IC03 Master Question Registry:** 112 exact historical question strings are included by author direction and attributed to *The Interrogative Conscience — Document 3, Final v1.0*. Source collection: https://zenodo.org/records/21818915. This inclusion does not license reproduction of unrelated IC03 material.
- **OIN-001-P:** 91 corresponding historical question strings are represented without alteration. The published OIN-001-P Final v1.0 has **CC BY-ND 4.0** terms; these do not extend to all data in this Crosswalk. No unadmitted candidate prompts are included. Source: https://aegissolisarchive.org/the-question-after-the-answer/
- **HFC-16M derivative:** The public Crosswalk uses catalog IDs, new editorial paraphrases, objection IDs, status labels, and typed mappings, not the original mixed-rights reviewer objections or reply prose. Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/AegisSolis/aegis-solis-hfc16m-public-beta
- **Other sources and URLs:** Catalog labels, retrieval routes, and metadata do not confer permission to republish the full linked works. Source-specific rights are retained; references to old branches or historical Coexilia are navigational, not re-opening or endorsing those archives.

**Held / excluded:** ARG-17 substantive argument text, the three held source objection texts, original HFC reviewer objection/reply prose, unadmitted OIN candidate prompts, private audit/review material, internal operational files, and non-public source documents. The Crosswalk contains **explicit held placeholders** for three ARG-17 objection slots, not the withheld substantive content.

**No blanket license.** This mixed-origin compilation is not licensed as a whole under CEPSAD's CC BY 4.0. Redistribution and future preservation destinations must honor each source's applicable rights. The original source RC9 ZIP is not included, and this note must not be called its byte-identical rights document.
